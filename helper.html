Patchcord Helper

A small free Mac app that lets Patchcord open every plugin you own. You install it once and forget it.

Download Patchcord Helper (free, for macOS 14 or later) Download coming soon

Why it's needed

Apps from the Mac App Store live in a sandbox: a safe space that keeps them out of the rest of your Mac. That's good for you, and Patchcord follows the rule.

But many plugins keep their license or sounds elsewhere on your Mac, in places a sandboxed app isn't allowed to look. Opened inside Patchcord, they can't find them, so they won't start. KORG's Gadget instruments are like this, and so are some big sample libraries.

Patchcord Helper runs those plugins outside the sandbox, the same way your DAW does, and hands them to Patchcord. Patchcord tells you when a plugin needs it; until then you don't need it at all.

What it does

  • When Patchcord can't open a plugin by itself, it starts Patchcord Helper and asks it to. The plugin then works like any other: your other devices play it, see its window and move its settings.
  • It remembers nothing of its own. Patchcord keeps the plugin's settings, as it does for every plugin.
  • It quits by itself a minute after Patchcord stops using it.

What it doesn't do

  • No window, no Dock icon, no menu. You won't see it unless you open a plugin's window.
  • Nothing unless Patchcord asks. It only listens to Patchcord on the same Mac, and opens only the plugin you chose.
  • Nothing goes to the internet. Like Patchcord, it sends a plugin's notes, sound and settings only between your own devices. No account, no analytics.
  • It doesn't change your plugins or anything else on your Mac.

Installing it

  1. Download it with the button above and open the file.
  2. Drag Patchcord Helper into your Applications folder.
  3. Back in Patchcord, press Try Again. That's all: Patchcord starts it whenever it's needed.

It's made by the maker of Patchcord and checked by Apple (notarized), so your Mac opens it without warnings.

The first time, your Mac asks twice

  • Local network: so your other devices can play the plugins it opens. Allow it.
  • Screen recording, only if you look at one of its plugins' windows from another device: so it can send that one window. Everything else works without it.

Removing it

Drag Patchcord Helper from Applications to the Trash. Patchcord goes back to opening only the plugins it can open itself.
