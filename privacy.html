Privacy

Updated November 23, 2026

The short version

Patchcord sends a plugin's notes, sound, settings and (when you ask) its window between your own devices, directly, on your network or over a USB-C cable. It has no account, no servers of its own, no analytics and no ads, and it sends nothing anywhere else.

What travels, and where

  • Between your devices only: the notes you play, the plugin's sound, its settings and presets, and the names of the plugins you choose to share. They go straight from one of your devices to another running Patchcord.
  • Its Screen: when you open a plugin's screen on your other device, the device that has the plugin sends pictures of that plugin's window only, and only while you're looking at it.
  • Nothing goes to the internet. Patchcord doesn't contact any website or server. (This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts; the app doesn't.)

What it asks your device for, and why

  • Local network: to find your other devices running Patchcord. It looks only for Patchcord.
  • Screen recording (Mac), or screen broadcast (iPad and iPhone): only for Its Screen, to send the plugin's own window to your other device. You're asked first, and you can say no; everything else still works.
  • Files: Record It Here saves your recordings on the device you're holding (in Files, On My iPad, Patchcord). They stay there unless you move them.

What it keeps

Your settings, snapshots and favorite plugins are saved on each device, in Patchcord's own storage. They aren't synced or sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Patchcord collects nothing from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
