Getting started

Open Patchcord on both devices. Tap How It Works on either one and it walks you through the first time: which device runs the plugin and which one sends the notes and hears it, choosing the plugin, picking it on the other device, and playing.

You can choose the plugin from either device. On the one you play from, open Play and press Choose a Plugin on your other device: it lists that device's plugins, opens the one you pick and shares it, with nobody touching it. Patchcord has to be open there.

My devices don't find each other

  • Both need Patchcord open, on the same Wi-Fi network, or joined by a USB-C cable.
  • Each device asks once to use the local network. If you said no, turn it on in Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, Patchcord.
  • Some guest and office networks keep devices from seeing each other. A USB-C cable between the two works anywhere.

It's linked, but there's no sound

  • Check Where its sound plays: Here brings it to the device you're holding, There leaves it on the device that runs the plugin.
  • Some iPad plugins need their own app opened once before they make sound anywhere. Open it, then try again.

Is it in time with my song?

Inside a DAW, yes: Patchcord Remote tells your DAW how far behind the sound is, and the DAW lines it up, so recorded and bounced tracks land on the grid. Played live, you hear each note a little after you play it, about as much as the line says under Details. A USB-C cable makes that the shortest.

A plugin won't open on the Mac

Patchcord from the App Store lives in Apple's sandbox, and some plugins keep their license or sounds where it isn't allowed to look. When Patchcord says a plugin needs Patchcord Helper, get the free helper from the Patchcord Helper page, put it in Applications and press Try Again. Patchcord starts it whenever it's needed.

Using it in a DAW

Put Patchcord Remote on an instrument track, or Patchcord FX on any track to run its sound through an effect on your other device. Both come with the app as Audio Units (AUv3) on the Mac and iPad. Choose the plugin from your other device in its window, and play from the track.

VST3, for Ableton Live with Audio Units turned off

The App Store can't install VST3 plugins, so they're a free download: Patchcord Remote and Patchcord FX, checked by Apple (notarized).

Download the VST3 plugins (free, for macOS 14 or later)

Open the file, then in Finder choose Go, Go to Folder, type ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and press Return. Drag both plugins into that folder, and rescan plugins in your DAW.

Sharing a synth from inside AUM

Put Patchcord Host after the synth on its channel in AUM, then route Patchcord Host's MIDI out to the synth. Name the channel in Patchcord Host's window; your other device sees it under Play by that name. Its window checks both steps: it says when it hears the synth, and Send a Test Note shows whether the MIDI route works.

Your other device's knobs send MIDI CCs (Cutoff on CC 74, Resonance on CC 71, and more, listed in its window), and Previous and Next Preset send Program Changes. Many synths follow Cutoff and Resonance by themselves; map the others in the synth or with AUM's MIDI control. It works in hosts that route a plugin's MIDI out (AUM, apeMatrix, Drambo); it can't show the synth's own window on the other device.

Effects after a plugin

Up to four effects can follow a shared plugin. They run on the device that runs the plugin, and its sound goes through them there before it comes to you. Add them on that device's Share card, or from the device you play it on with the button under the plugin's name (Add Effects After It...): it lists the effects on the other device. Their settings join the plugin's own, under Knobs and All Settings, and each one's window has its own button on Its Screen. A plugin opened by Patchcord Helper can have effects after it too, with the newest helper.

Its Screen

  • A Mac plugin on the iPad: the Mac asks once to let Patchcord record the screen. Allow it, then reopen Patchcord on the Mac: macOS only lets an app record after it starts again. Choose Quit & Reopen if macOS offers it, or press Reopen Patchcord in Patchcord. A plugin opened by Patchcord Helper needs Patchcord Helper turned on too, in the same list (System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen & System Audio Recording). Only that plugin's window is sent, and you can touch it from the iPad.
  • An iPad plugin on the Mac: the iPad asks each time; tap Allow on the iPad. From an iPad you can look but not touch (iPadOS doesn't allow it), so turn its knobs with Knobs or All Settings.
