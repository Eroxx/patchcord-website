Two devices, never one instrument.

Every device you own has sounds the others can't reach. Patchcord joins them with a cord you never have to plug in.

Nine ways to use it.

iPad synth → Ableton on the Mac

An iPad synth in your song.

Put Patchcord Remote on a track in Ableton and pick Animoog on the iPad. Play it from the track: its sound comes back into Live, in time, ready to record.

Mac plugin ← iPad on the couch

The big plugins, from anywhere in the room.

Omnisphere stays on the Mac. Play it from the iPad, see its own window there, and let its sound come out of the Mac's speakers.

iPhone → any synth

Your phone as a control surface.

Set where its sound plays to Neither: settings only, and the iPhone becomes knobs, an XY pad and snapshots for a synth on your Mac or iPad, while you play it from its own keyboard.

Mac keys → iPad window

A second screen for one plugin.

Play on the Mac and keep the plugin's window on the iPad beside you, where you can touch it: no window shuffling over your song.

Any device → a recording

Catch the idea where you are.

Play an iPad synth from your phone and press Record It Here: a 24-bit recording lands on the device you're holding.

One Mac → a live rig

Four synths, one tablet on stage.

Share four plugins from the Mac, switch between them on the iPad in Perform, and jump between sounds with snapshots.

Mac track → iPad delay → back

The delay on the iPad, the synth on the Mac.

Put Patchcord FX on a track in your DAW and pick an effect shared from the iPad. The track's sound goes through it and comes back, lined up with the rest of your song.

Synth + effects → one sound

A synth with its own effects.

Put a reverb, a delay or a filter after the shared synth, on the device that runs it. Add, reorder or turn them off from either device, open each one's own window, and turn every knob in the chain.

Your project → the iPad's sound

Open the song, get the sound back.

Your DAW project keeps how the synth on the other device was set. Open it next month and Patchcord puts that sound back on the iPad.

Patchcord Remote

A plugin that plays a plugin on your other device.

Put Patchcord Remote on a track like any instrument. Your DAW's notes play the synth on your iPad, its sound comes back into the track, and its settings show up as parameters you can automate, map to a controller or move with an LFO.

For effects there's Patchcord FX: put it on any track, and the track's sound goes through an effect on your other device and comes back.

And on the device with the synth, Patchcord Host shares it from inside AUM: put it after the synth on its channel, and your other device plays that synth.

  • In time with your track. Patchcord tells your DAW how far behind the sound is, and the DAW lines it up.
  • Notes keep their spacing, to the sample, however busy the Wi-Fi is.
  • Bounces work. An offline export waits for every block of sound instead of leaving gaps.
  • 32 settings slots, named after what they hold, saved with your project.
  • Your project keeps the sound. How the plugin on the other device was set is saved with the song, and put back when you open it.

AUv3 on Mac and iPad, and VST3 on the Mac, a free download. Tried in Ableton Live.

Its Screen

The plugin's own window, on your other device.

Open a Mac plugin's real window on your iPad and turn its knobs with your finger, and the window of each effect after it too. From an iPad, the Mac sees the plugin's screen live, to watch while you play.

Made to play, not just to connect.

Knobs that move themselves

Eight knobs, each with Motion: an LFO in time with your song, random steps, following its sound, or swelling on every note.

An XY pad

Turn two settings at once with one finger.

Perform, full screen

Big knobs and keys, nothing else, for playing live.

Snapshots and Morph

Save how it sounds to a pad, tap to bring it all back, or slide between two.

Its presets

Step through the plugin's own presets from the other device.

Every setting by name

All of them, in the plugin's own words, searchable, in groups.

MIDI keyboards

Play with a keyboard on either device. Turn a hardware knob to link it.

Record it here

A 24-bit recording of what you played, on the device you're holding.

Four at once, with effects

Share up to four plugins from one device, each with up to four effects after it, set up from either device.

Wi-Fi or USB-C

Over your network, or plug in a USB-C cable: Patchcord uses it on its own, and the sound arrives in about 18 ms, against about 100 over Wi-Fi.

Nothing leaves your room

Your devices talk to each other directly. No account, no cloud.
